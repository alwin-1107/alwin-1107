### 👋 About Me

Graduate Software Engineer who likes making machines *see* and understand the physical world. I build things at the intersection of **machine learning**, **computer vision**, and **real-time web engineering** — with a soft spot for shipping without heavyweight SDKs when a browser can do the job instead.

- 🔭 Currently building **RoomScan** — a browser-only WebAR tool that measures real objects and drops a to-scale 3D twin into your room, no app install required
- 🎓 B.E. Information Technology @ FCRIT, Navi Mumbai — Class of 2026
- 🌱 Currently sharpening up on **AWS** and AI fundamentals (certs in progress)
- 💬 Ask me about ML pipelines, WebAR, or why a gyroscope alone can't give you 6DoF
- 📫 alwinmathew1107@gmail.com — always up for a good conversation about tech
